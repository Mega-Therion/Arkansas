03Cloud — the civic fruit

What the tree is actually for

The commercial layer exists to fund this layer, not the other way around. Three bills, one target: pre-filing November 2026, for Arkansas's 2027 Regular Legislative Session.

Bill

ARMAWS

Arkansas Rural Mobility Air Standard — guarantees every traveler free, mechanically pressurized air to fill their tires. Modeled on Connecticut's §14‑325a, 47 years durable: no purchase required, mandatory signage, ≥80 psi. State-surplus funded, closing the one legitimate objection (electricity and upkeep) that undid California's fuel-purchase-conditional version.

● pre-filing target: nov 2026
Bill

Driver's-Test Reform

Asset-barrier reform companion bill: state-provided testing fleets and fee deferment, so the cost of a working vehicle stops gatekeeping the right to legally drive to work.

Program

Project RENEW

ADC reentry riding the commercial branches directly — real jobs inside recoveRY, refineRY, deliveRY, and the rest, for people coming out of the Arkansas Department of Corrections.

“Two bald tires and a plug in the third, while the fourth has a slow leak they have to wait until payday to afford to patch — so they're putting air in every other day just to keep getting to work.”

That asymmetry — a few dollars of air against a lost job, a missed shift, a $200 tow, a highway blowout — is the whole economic and moral case. It's one real person, not an abstraction, and it's who ARMAWS is written for.
