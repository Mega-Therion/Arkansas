# House District 86 Legislative Liaison Dossier: Al Martin & Rep. John Maddox

## 1. Executive Summary

This  dossier formalizes the strategic constituent liaison  pathway  for the  **Entergy  Rate Stability (ERS)** bill proposal and **Rate Stabilization Fund (RSF)** under APSC Docket `26-001-U`.

   - Restoring balanced regulatory safeguards to Ark. Code Ann. § 23-18-1001 et seq. (Act 373 / SB307).
