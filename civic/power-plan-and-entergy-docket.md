# The power plan & the Entergy ratepayer fight

**Status:** manifesto stage — not yet drafted as statute text, not yet filed as public comment
**Context:** APSC Docket No. 26-001-U (fixed-customer-charge analysis, Entergy Arkansas rate case)

## The problem, in RY's own framing

Arkansas households can't choose their power provider, monopoly territory or not. The bill jumps
regardless, and the state approved every charge that produced the jump. The core claim: the state
either takes responsibility for its citizens or for the power companies that serve the state —
preferably both — using tax money Arkansans already pay in, on the back end.

Arkansas Nuclear One's fuel costs get passed through to residential bills via the fuel-adjustment
clause, and plant upgrades get billed to citizens under the GAJA rider — the same plant that ONE
Natural Energy (the commercial-layer trunk) is built to monetize at the fence line. The civic-layer
fix and the commercial-layer trunk are framed as two sides of the same relationship with that one
plant, not separate ideas.

## The fix — Rate Stabilization Fund

State surplus stands in front of the ratepayer: a rollover fund absorbs the increase first, unused
margin gets eaten before the citizen does. Funded from general-revenue surplus — no new tax,
deterministic cost, same "state pays first" principle as ARMAWS and the License Fleet.

## What's still open

- The Rate Stabilization Fund has not yet been written as actual statute text.
- No public comment has been filed on Docket No. 26-001-U yet.
- The plan has not yet been added as the third pillar of the Arkancentric legislative hub
  alongside ARMAWS and the License Fleet act (as of the last working session on this).
