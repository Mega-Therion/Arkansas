# Sovereign Arkansas Defense Posture

**Classification:** Economic Sovereignty & Defense Infrastructure
**Status:** Directive ratified by R.W. Yett 2026-10-21 ("i spake them"). Standing crew program. This is a posture document, not a bill draft — no statutory text exists yet.
**Target:** framework for Arkansas's defense-industrial positioning; procurement alignment with federal dispersal programs.

Arkansas owns its defense ecosystem. Not hosts it — owns it.

## The four pillars

**1. Sovereign capacity.** Arkansas entities own the IP, the prime contracts, and the value chain. No absentee primes extracting value while Arkansas supplies only land and labor.

**2. Arkansas-refined inputs.** Smackover brine/lithium and quartz/silicon refined in-state. Raw materials leave Arkansas as finished inputs, not as ore.

**3. Behind-the-meter baseload.** Defense and industrial facilities carry their own baseload behind the meter — residential ratepayers are never burdened. Under Act 373. This is the same anti-risk-shifting principle as the [Rate Stabilization Fund](power-plan-and-entergy-docket.md): Arkansans don't subsidize industrial load.

**4. Matching procurement language.** FAR 19.7 / NDAA dispersal language, so the posture is legible to the federal procurement system it intends to serve.
