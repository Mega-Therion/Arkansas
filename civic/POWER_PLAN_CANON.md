# Power plan: RY's rules (check every draft against this)

Source: [`power-manifesto-in-his-words.md`](power-manifesto-in-his-words.md) and RY, 2026-10-20. When a draft, model, or agent take disagrees with this list, the draft is wrong. A model result can change *how* a rule is written. It never removes a rule.

1. **A hard, honest rate** for the power you actually used.
2. **The citizen is never the first wallet.** That holds for any extra cost: fuel, storm, tornado, any isolated event.
3. **Order of payment:**
   1. The state surplus fund, set aside from Arkansas's surplus budget, pays first.
   2. The system's own slack pays second: the utility's own operating money, and unused usage margin. Households that used less than their average are never charged extra.
   3. Interest earned by the fund pays third.
   4. Last resort only: a tiny remainder spread statewide per kWh, so a disaster in one region (NW Arkansas tornado) is shared a cent or two by every county, Delta included.
4. **Rollover:** good months carry the rough ones. Unspent money stays in the fund.
5. **Hard cap** on how much a bill can rise from a surprise.
6. **Transparent**, so any citizen can follow it without being a lawyer or a lobbyist.
7. **The fund is spent when needed.** It is not an endowment and it is not a subsidy for building plants. New plant for new large load is paid by that load (Sen. Rice's shared ground: residential customers don't foot the data-center bill).
8. **The company still runs and the lights stay on.**
