# MODEL BILL: Arkansas Residential Rate Stabilization Fund

*Model text for BLR and counsel review. Not filed. Built from RY's rules in [`../power-manifesto-in-his-words.md`](../power-manifesto-in-his-words.md). See [`README.md`](README.md).*

**AN ACT TO CREATE THE RESIDENTIAL RATE STABILIZATION FUND, SO THAT EXTRAORDINARY RESIDENTIAL ELECTRIC RATE ADJUSTMENTS ARE ABSORBED FIRST BY STATE SURPLUS AND BY UNUSED SYSTEM MARGIN BEFORE THEY ARE BILLED TO HOUSEHOLDS; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.**

**SECTION 1. Title.** "Arkansas Residential Rate Stabilization Act".

**SECTION 2. Findings.** The General Assembly finds that:
(1) Residential customers of a public utility with an exclusive service territory cannot choose their electric provider;
(2) Riders and adjustments approved by the state can raise every residential bill at once, regardless of how much power a household used;
(3) A fixed customer charge raises the bills of low-usage households the most; and
(4) The state carries general revenue surpluses in many years, including $655 million at the close of fiscal year 2026.

**SECTION 3. Definitions.**
(1) "Extraordinary adjustment" means an increase in a residential charge, other than in the per-kilowatt-hour base energy rate, that the Arkansas Public Service Commission approves outside a general rate case, or a fixed-charge increase approved in a general rate case, that raises the average residential bill by more than [__] percent in a twelve-month period;
(2) "Fund" means the Residential Rate Stabilization Fund.

**SECTION 4. Fund created.**
(a) There is created in the State Treasury the Residential Rate Stabilization Fund.
(b) The fund consists of amounts transferred from general revenue surplus as provided by the Revenue Stabilization Law, and of interest earned. The fund balance may not exceed [__].
(c) Unspent balances roll over from month to month and from year to year.

**SECTION 5. Order of payment.** When the Commission approves an extraordinary adjustment, the residential portion shall be paid in this order:
(1) **First,** by the fund, up to the monthly draw limit set by the Commission;
(2) **Second,** by offsetting it against the unused usage margin of residential customers in the same rate class whose consumption fell below their own twelve-month average, so that customers who used less are not charged for a cost they did not cause **[VERIFY the methodology with Commission staff]**;
(3) **Last,** any remainder is spread across the residential class per kilowatt-hour, not per customer.

**SECTION 6. Hard cap.** No residential customer's bill shall rise in any month because of extraordinary adjustments by more than [$__ or __%]. Amounts above the cap are deferred and recovered under Section 5.

**SECTION 7. Transparency.** Each month the Commission shall publish, in plain language: the adjustments in effect, the amount paid by the fund, the amount offset under Section 5(2), the amount billed to customers, and the fund balance.

**SECTION 8. Not a subsidy of utility returns.** Nothing in this act changes the Commission's duty to set just and reasonable rates under § 23-4-101 et seq. The fund pays costs the Commission has already found reasonable. It never raises an allowed return.

**SECTION 9. Review.** The Commission shall report after three years on whether the fund reduced bill volatility or only delayed increases, and shall recommend whether to continue it.
