# MODEL BILL: Driver's License Public Access Guarantee Act

*Model text for BLR and counsel review. Not filed. See [`README.md`](README.md).*

**AN ACT TO ENSURE THAT AN APPLICANT FOR A DRIVER'S LICENSE IS NOT REQUIRED TO PROVIDE A PRIVATELY OWNED VEHICLE IN ORDER TO TAKE THE SKILLS EXAMINATION; TO AUTHORIZE A STATE TESTING FLEET; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.**

**SECTION 1. Title.** "Driver's License Public Access Guarantee Act".

**SECTION 2. Findings.** The General Assembly finds that:
(1) Arkansas Code § 27-16-704 requires an applicant to demonstrate the ability to operate a motor vehicle but does not require the applicant to supply the vehicle;
(2) Requiring an applicant to supply a registered and insured vehicle keeps qualified people from being licensed, and therefore from working, particularly in rural counties; and
(3) The District of Columbia provides a road-test vehicle at no additional fee, showing that a state-provided test vehicle is practical.

**SECTION 3. Amendment.** Arkansas Code § 27-16-704 is amended to add:
"(__) The state shall not require an applicant to provide a privately owned vehicle as a prerequisite to completing the skills examination. An applicant may use the applicant's own properly registered and insured vehicle, or a testing vehicle provided under § 27-16-7__."

**SECTION 4. Testing fleet.** Arkansas Code Title 27, Chapter 16, Subchapter 7 is amended to add § 27-16-7__:
(a) The Division of Arkansas State Police shall maintain testing vehicles sufficient to offer the skills examination, on a scheduled basis, in each county where the examination is given.
(b) Vehicles may be acquired under the Automobile and Pickup Truck Acquisition Act, § 22-8-201 et seq., including from state surplus before public sale.
(c) Testing vehicles shall be insured under the state's vehicle risk-management program. **[VERIFY the correct citation; § 21-5-701 is unconfirmed.]**
(d) An applicant who uses a testing vehicle may be required to sign a standard consent and release form. No additional fee shall be charged.

**SECTION 5. Pilot first.** The Division shall begin with a pilot of not fewer than five (5) and not more than ten (10) vehicles in a rural troop area, and expand statewide no later than [date].

**SECTION 6. Reporting.** The Division shall report to the House and Senate transportation committees each [six months] on tests given in testing vehicles, pass rates, cost per test, and the counties served.

**SECTION 7. Appropriation note.** Act 1006 of 2025 (HB 1202), § 24, appropriated $15,000,000 for vehicle purchases from the Motor Vehicle Acquisition Revolving Fund for fiscal year 2025–2026. A 2027 implementation requires a current-year appropriation. **[Coordinate with the DFA – Disbursing Officer appropriation bill.]**
