# MODEL BILL: Arkansas Infant Nutrition Security Act (AINSA)

*Model text for BLR and counsel review. Not filed. See [`README.md`](README.md).*

**AN ACT TO CREATE A STATE INFANT NUTRITION BRIDGE THAT COVERS THE GAP BETWEEN THE FEDERAL WIC FORMULA ALLOWANCE AND AN INFANT'S DOCUMENTED NEED; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.**

**SECTION 1. Title.** "Arkansas Infant Nutrition Security Act".

**SECTION 2. Findings.** The General Assembly finds that:
(1) Federal regulation, 7 CFR § 246.10, limits the infant formula issued under WIC each month;
(2) For heavier infants in the first months of life, that limit can fall several days short of the infant's appetite at the end of the month;
(3) Families who run short are at risk of diluting formula, which can seriously harm an infant; and
(4) The state cannot change the federal allowance, but it can fund a separate state supplement.

**SECTION 3. Infant Nutrition Bridge.**
(a) The Department of Health, as the state WIC agency, shall provide a monthly state formula supplement to a WIC participant whose infant is fully formula-fed.
(b) The supplement is the lesser of:
  (1) the infant's documented need, up to thirty-two (32) fluid ounces per day, minus the federal formula actually issued that month; or
  (2) [__] days of formula at the infant's documented daily need.
(c) The supplement shall be issued on the participant's existing WIC benefit card where practicable, and may be redeemed at any authorized WIC retailer for any infant formula the Department approves, without regard to the federal contract brand.
(d) Amounts above the standard need schedule require the medical documentation already used for WIC tailoring.

**SECTION 4. Separate from federal WIC.** The bridge is a state program funded with state money. Nothing in this act changes any federal WIC requirement. **[VERIFY with USDA FNS that card-based issuance of a state-funded supplement is permitted, or specify a separate state EBT benefit.]**

**SECTION 5. Funding.** The bridge is funded by appropriation from general revenue. No new tax is levied.

**SECTION 6. Automatic sunset and shrink.** If federal allowances increase, the bridge shrinks by the same amount. The bridge expires if the federal allowance meets the need schedule in Section 3.

**SECTION 7. Reporting.** The Department shall report annually on the number of infants served, the average ounces supplied, cost, and the distribution by formula form.
