# Model bill language: drafts for counsel

**What these are:** working model text, drafted 2026-10-07 by Claude (an AI assistant) from R.W. Yett's policy designs in `../`. They exist so that something concrete can be marked up. They are **not** RY's words, **not** legal advice, and **not** filed.

**Before any of these goes to a legislator:**
1. The Bureau of Legislative Research (BLR) redrafts them into official form; Arkansas bills are drafted by BLR on a member's request.
2. An Arkansas-licensed attorney reviews the funding sections against each fund's statutory dedication.
3. Every `[VERIFY]` marker is resolved.
