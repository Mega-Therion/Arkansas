# MODEL BILL: Arkansas Rural Mobility Air Standard (ARMAWS)

*Model text for BLR and counsel review. Not filed. See [`README.md`](README.md).*

**AN ACT TO REQUIRE RETAIL MOTOR FUEL DISPENSING FACILITIES TO PROVIDE FREE COMPRESSED AIR FOR TIRE INFLATION; TO CREATE THE RURAL MOBILITY AIR FUND; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.**

**SECTION 1. Title.** This act shall be known and may be cited as the "Arkansas Rural Mobility Air Standard".

**SECTION 2. Findings.** The General Assembly finds that:
(1) Properly inflated tires are a basic condition of safe highway travel and of reliable transportation to work;
(2) Charging for compressed air causes drivers with the least money to delay inflating tires, which increases blowouts, stopping distances, crashes, and tire waste;
(3) Connecticut has required free compressed air at retail fuel stations since 1979 (Conn. Gen. Stat. § 14-325a) without purchase conditions, and the requirement has proven durable; and
(4) A requirement tied to a fuel purchase has proven difficult to enforce, and this act therefore requires no purchase.

**SECTION 3. Definitions.** As used in this act:
(1) "Retail motor fuel facility" means a facility that sells motor fuel to the public at retail;
(2) "Air equipment" means an operable compressor and hose capable of delivering at least eighty pounds per square inch (80 psi), with a gauge; and
(3) "Department" means the Department of Agriculture, Bureau of Standards.

**SECTION 4. Free air required.**
(a) During business hours, every retail motor fuel facility that has air equipment, or that is required to install it under Section 6, shall make the air available free of charge to any person inflating the tires of a motor vehicle, without requiring a purchase.
(b) The facility shall post a conspicuous sign, visible from the fuel dispensers, stating: "FREE AIR FOR TIRES — NO PURCHASE REQUIRED."
(c) A facility may use a coin or card device only if the device is set to operate at no charge.

**SECTION 5. Enforcement.**
(a) The Department shall enforce this act as part of its inspections under the Uniform Weights and Measures Law, Arkansas Code § 4-18-301 et seq., and shall maintain a public complaint line.
(b) Penalties: a written warning for a first violation; a civil penalty of up to [$___] for a second violation within twelve (12) months; and up to [$___] for each later violation within twelve (12) months. **[VERIFY penalty amounts against comparable Bureau of Standards schedules.]**
(c) A facility is not in violation while air equipment is out of service, if the outage is posted and the equipment is repaired within [__] days.

**SECTION 6. Rural Mobility Air Fund.**
(a) There is created the Rural Mobility Air Fund, to be administered by the Department.
(b) The fund shall consist of appropriations from general revenue surplus and any other moneys lawfully transferred to it. **[VERIFY whether any portion of used-tire fee revenue under § 8-9-401 et seq. may lawfully be transferred, given its statutory dedication.]**
(c) The fund shall pay up to one hundred percent (100%) of the purchase, installation, and signage cost of air equipment for an independent retail motor fuel facility that has fewer than [__] locations.
(d) **Safe harbor.** A facility that has applied for a grant under subsection (c) is immune from penalties under Section 5 until the grant is decided and, if awarded, for [__] days after the funds are received.

**SECTION 7. Rural service points.** Where no retail motor fuel facility is within [__] miles, the Department may certify a public site, such as a library, cooperative facility, county yard, or mobile unit, as a free air service point, and the fund may pay for its equipment.

**SECTION 8. Privacy.** No air equipment funded under this act may collect, store, or sell information identifying a person or a vehicle's location. Anonymous counts of use are permitted.

**SECTION 9. Reporting.** The Department shall report annually to the Legislative Council on complaints, violations, grants awarded, service points certified, and fund balance.

**SECTION 10. Effective date.** [January 1, 2028], to give retailers time to apply for grants.
