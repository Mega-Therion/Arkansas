# Project RENEW

**Full name:** RENEW, *Rehabilitative Employment through Natural Environment Work*. It also appears as "ADC Inmate Remediation, AI Environmental Work & Reentry Guarantee".
**Classification:** Criminal Justice Reform, Environmental Remediation & Workforce Reentry
**Sponsoring entity:** foundRY, with a dedicated 501(c)(3) as the program's fiscal host
**Status:** a full written program document exists (2026-03-02 proposal, expanded in the 2026-08 Entergy/RENEW session). It is not yet statute text and has not been piloted.

> ⚠ **Number discipline.** The Wholonomy's Part IX ([`../wholonomy/the-arkansas-orchard-wholonomy.md`](../wholonomy/the-arkansas-orchard-wholonomy.md)) and the Manus offshoots report §7 both rule that RENEW's dollar, recidivism, employment, scrap-revenue and 40/30/30 figures **must not appear in public-facing material until a voluntary, independently monitored pilot produces them**. They're kept below, labeled `[O]`, only so the planning record is complete.

## The converging crises

- **Recidivism trap** `[O]`:
  - roughly 50% of people released return within 3 years;
  - more than 60% of admissions are supervision revocations;
  - housing about 16,663 people costs an estimated $452.9M a year.
- **Litter burden** `[O]`: ARDOT is estimated to spend about $5M a year picking up about 32 million pieces of highway litter.
- **ADC's own stated direction** (cited in the Manus report): reentry starts at incarceration and ends with successful reintegration, and workforce partnerships are central to reducing recidivism.

## The design (the version that survived review)

The Wholonomy (Part V) and the offshoots report §3.10 tightened the original idea into these rules:

1. **Truly voluntary.** No participation pressured by deprivation. A grievance process run by an **independent auditor**, not the operator. Basic rights are never conditioned on working.
2. **Ordinary wage rules.**
   - At least Arkansas minimum wage ($11/hr in 2025) or prevailing wage.
   - Deductions bounded and disclosed.
   - Federal PIECP, 18 U.S.C. § 1761(c), caps deductions where it applies. The 80% figure needs re-verifying.
3. **Escrow controlled by an independent fiduciary**, not foundRY. The goal is to end "day-1 poverty" on release.
   - Proposed split `[O]`, not a legal template: 40% release escrow / 30% family support or restitution / 30% commissary.
4. **Competency credentials owned by the worker.** A libraRY ledger entry records the skill demonstrated, the supervisor's sign-off and employer-verified readiness. The worker keeps it.
5. **Real employers, not promises.** "Guaranteed employment" applies only where an employer signs a conditional offer.
   - Pathways: environmental services, CDL and fleet work (deliveRY), materials sorting (recoveRY), electrical and solar maintenance (ONE), CNC and welding (factoRY), data-center physical security.
6. **A 90-day transport guarantee** after release, to work, licensing appointments and services. This links to the license fleet ([`drivers-license-reform.md`](drivers-license-reform.md)).
7. **Heat and safety protections.** Hydration and heat-index monitoring are built into crew routing.

## The AI layer, and its privacy wall

- **OmegA** (Chyren's predecessor) handles analytics only: route optimization toward dense litter, anomaly detection and outcome tracking.
- It works on **pseudonymized tokens only** (for example `human_1@!903`).
- **ADC alone holds the identity mapping**, and it is never shared with OmegA or ONE.
- **ONE** provides the physical layer: a "Bilocal Transport Network" between facilities and work corridors, plus hardened data storage and a security-training pipeline.

## Arkansas law it builds on (checked)

- **Act 670 of 2025** (SB 485) ✓: supervision reform and a single evidence-based-practices unit inside the Department of Corrections. Tying RENEW's earned-release credits to Act 670 is **the plan's design claim** and hasn't been checked against the act's text.
- **Act 927 of 2025** (HB 1876, signed 2025-04-21) ✓: ownership of generative-AI output and trained models. Whoever supplies the lawfully obtained inputs owns the output; under work-for-hire, the employer owns it. This bears on licensing RENEW's telemetry-trained models. An earlier claimed "re-identification ban" in Act 927 **could not be confirmed and was removed**.
- **Act 848 of 2025** (HB 1958, approved 4/17/25; amends Ark. Code Ann. § 25-1-128) ✓ read in the act text. Effective date reported as 2025-08-03 (search summary, not read in a primary source). **every Arkansas public entity, ADC included, must adopt a written AI policy** with **human final decision-making authority**, staff training and public availability. Any OmegA analytics used by ADC have to sit inside ADC's Act 848 policy.
- **PIECP**, 18 U.S.C. § 1761(c): wage and deduction limits if prison-industry certification applies. Counsel must map this before any program starts.

## Revenue lines in the plan (all `[O]`)

- Scrap aluminum from litter: $3k–$31k per highway route, $21k–$190k statewide.
- An offset against ARDOT's litter cost.
- Environmental and carbon-offset payments.
- AI data licensing under Act 927.
- ONE partnership revenue.
- An estimated fiscal dividend of $18.5M–$34M a year from reduced prison-bed demand.
- Post-release 90-day employment raised from under 35% to over 78%.

## Bill concept (from the offshoots report, `[conj]`)

**Remediation Work Rights and Safety Act:**
- voluntary participation;
- wage and safety floors;
- heat and medical protections;
- grievance access;
- a ban on conditioning basic rights on labor participation.
