# Project RENEW

**Full name:** ADC Inmate Remediation, AI Environmental Work & Reentry Guarantee
**Classification:** Criminal Justice Reform, Environmental Remediation & Workforce Reentry
**Sponsoring entity:** foundRY
**Status:** full written report exists, not yet drafted as statute text

## The converging crises it's built to answer

- **Recidivism trap:** ~50% of released individuals return to Arkansas prisons within 3 years;
  over 60% of admissions are supervision revocations, not new crimes. Housing ~16,663 individuals
  costs Arkansas taxpayers an estimated **$452.9M/year** (~$74+/day per inmate).
- **Environmental burden:** ARDOT spends an estimated **$5M/year** picking up ~32 million pieces
  of highway litter (~2,000 pieces/mile).

## The operational engine

1. **Compensated, voluntary work crews** — eligible ADC volunteers earn wages performing highway
   litter removal, illegal tire-dump cleanup, and recyclable sorting.
2. **OmegA AI intelligence layer** — autonomous drone/satellite mapping routes crews to
   high-density litter zones, monitors live hydration/heat-index safety, tracks chain of custody
   for recovered metals, polymers, and rubber.
3. **The escrow wealth engine** (aimed at eliminating "day-1 poverty" on release):
   - **40%** → mandatory reentry escrow, locked until release — exits prison with an estimated
     **$3,000–$10,000** in liquid capital for first/last month's rent, transportation, trade tools.
   - **30%** → family support / victim restitution.
   - **30%** → in-custody commissary / discretionary spending.
4. **Guaranteed employment bridge** — direct pipeline into deliveRY (CDL/fleet operations),
   recoveRY (materials sorting), and ONE (solar/field electrical maintenance). Estimated to raise
   90-day post-release employment from under 35% to over 78%.
5. **Fiscal dividend** — estimated **$18.5M–$34.0M/year** in net taxpayer savings, from reduced
   prison-bed demand and lower recidivism.

## Where it sits in the portfolio

RENEW is described as the labor pipeline and moral center of the whole Orchard: it takes someone
inside the cage and hands them work, savings, and a door out, then feeds them directly into the
commercial branches (deliveRY, recoveRY, ONE) rather than back into the system.

All dollar figures above are estimates from the source planning document, not audited numbers —
carry the same caveat when citing externally.

## Compliance & funding detail

- **Three-way integration**, more specifically: OmegA (route optimization/analytics) +
  ONE's Bilocal Transport Network (logistics) + ONE's data-center physical-security jobs
  (employment pipeline) — RENEW is the labor bridge across all three, not a standalone program.
- **Wage floor:** must meet Arkansas minimum wage (**$11/hr, 2025**) or prevailing wage, whichever
  applies to the work performed.
- **Federal PIECP cap:** the Prison Industry Enhancement Certification Program caps permissible
  deductions at **80% of gross wages** — the escrow/restitution/commissary split above has to fit
  inside that ceiling.
- **Earned-Release Credits** are tied to **Arkansas Act 670**.
- **Vocational certifications** issued: logistics, environmental services, data-center security.
- **Scrap-aluminum revenue** (from the litter-collection work crews): estimated
  **$3,000–$31,000 per highway route**, **$21,000–$190,000 statewide** — separate from and
  additive to the $5M/year ARDOT litter-cost offset above.
- **Additional revenue lines:** environmental/carbon-offset payments; a dedicated 501(c)(3) as the
  program's fiscal host; AI data licensing revenue via OmegA, governed by **Act 927**, which sets
  the rules for the entity owning trained models and bans re-identification of the underlying data.
