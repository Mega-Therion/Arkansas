## 1. Civic Layer Statutory & Code Verification

### Part I: ARMAWS (Arkansas Rural Mobility Air Standard)
*   **Repo Doc:** [`civic/armaws.md`](civic/armaws.md)
*   **Core Mandate:** Guarantees free compressed tire air at retail motor-fuel dispensaries, and prohibits paywalled air compressors ($0.75–$3.00).
*   **Statutory Alignment:**
    *   **Primary Legislative Model:** Connecticut General Statutes **§ 14-325a** (*Air compressors*). It requires licensed motor-fuel retailers to provide an air compressor **for free public use** during business hours, capable of at least **80 psi**, with a conspicuous sign. A small-retail-food-store exemption (< 10,000 gal/month) has since been narrowed.
    *   **Contrast:** California Business & Professions Code **§ 13651** ties free air and water to customers who purchase fuel, which leaves an enforcement gap ARMAWS is meant to avoid. *(This characterization of § 13651 was not re-verified in this pass.)*
    *   **Enforcement Authority:** Arkansas Department of Agriculture, **Bureau of Standards, Division of Weights and Measures**, under the Uniform Weights and Measures Law, **Ark. Code Ann. § 4-18-301 et seq.**
    *   **Funding Mechanism & Takings Safe Harbor:** Draws on used-tire fee revenue under the **Used Tire Recycling and Accountability Act**, **Ark. Code Ann. § 8-9-401 et seq.** (Act 317 of 2017). That revenue is administered through ADEQ's Tire Accountability Program (TAP) and Pollution Control & Ecology Commission Rule 36. The model is a 100% equipment-retrofit grant for independent retailers, so the mandate is funded rather than unfunded.

### Part II: Driver's License Public Access Guarantee Act
*   **Repo Doc:** [`civic/drivers-license-reform.md`](civic/drivers-license-reform.md)
*   **Core Mandate:** Ends the "Mobility Trap", in which applicants must bring a registered, insured private vehicle to the skills test.
*   **Statutory Alignment:**
    *   **State Law vs. Administrative Rule:** **Ark. Code Ann. § 27-16-704** (*Examinations of applicants*) requires an eyesight test, a knowledge test, and "an actual demonstration of the applicant's ability to exercise ordinary and reasonable control in the operation of a motor vehicle". **§ 27-16-705** (*Examiners*) assigns examinations to the Arkansas State Police or authorized DFA agents. Neither section says who supplies the vehicle.
    *   **Fleet Sourcing & Existing Revolving Fund:** Under the *Automobile and Pickup Truck Acquisition Act for the State of Arkansas* (**Ark. Code Ann. § 22-8-201 et seq.**; purchasing under **§ 22-8-206**), the DFA Director buys state vehicles from the Motor Vehicle Acquisition Revolving Fund.
    *   **Fiscal Scope:** Proposes surplus state units before public auction, plus forfeited vehicles under **Ark. Code Ann. § 5-64-505**. That section now forbids forfeiture without a related felony conviction, so "civilly forfeited" is not accurate. Liability coverage is proposed under the state vehicle risk-management program; the cited **§ 21-5-701 et seq.** was **not verified** in this pass.

### Part III: AINSA (Arkansas Infant Nutrition Security Act)
*   **Repo Doc:** [`civic/infant-nutrition-security-act.md`](civic/infant-nutrition-security-act.md)
*   **Core Mandate:** Eliminates the "Month-End Formula Gap" for rural infants on WIC.
*   **Federal & State Regulatory Grounding:**
    *   **Federal MMA Regulation:** **7 CFR § 246.10**, Table 1, Food Package I. For fully formula-fed infants aged 0–3 months, the maximum monthly allowance is **806 fl oz reconstituted liquid concentrate, or 832 fl oz ready-to-feed, or 870 fl oz reconstituted powder**. Powder is issued in whole 12.4-oz cans of about 90 fl oz each. A 10th can would exceed the 870 cap, so the actual powder benefit is 9 cans, **~810 fl oz**. The benefit actually issued is therefore 806 (concentrate), 832 (ready-to-feed) or ~810 (powder). At 30 oz/day the monthly gap is 94 / 68 / 90 fl oz (3.1 / 2.3 / 3.0 days). See the form-by-form table in [`civic/infant-nutrition-security-act.md`](civic/infant-nutrition-security-act.md).
    *   **Actual Pediatric Appetite:** The commonly cited American Academy of Pediatrics guideline is about 2.5 oz per pound of body weight per day, capped near 32 oz/day.
    *   **Sole-Source Distortion:** The repo cites USDA ERS reports on WIC sole-source rebates and GAO findings on market share and price spillover. *These figures were not re-verified in this pass.*
    *   **Statutory Honesty Clause:** AINSA does not alter 7 CFR § 246.10, which would need a federal waiver. It funds an independent **State Supplemental Infant Nutrition Bridge Fund** through Arkansas DHS. The state-law anchor cited, **Ark. Code Ann. § 20-7-101 et seq.**, was **not verified** in this pass.

### Part IV: Project RENEW (ADC Reentry & Environmental Remediation)
*   **Repo Doc:** [`civic/project-renew.md`](civic/project-renew.md)
*   **Core Mandate:** Compensated, voluntary Arkansas Division of Correction (ADC) work crews for highway litter removal and materials recovery. Wages split into a 40% release escrow, 30% restitution/family and 30% commissary.
*   **Statutory Alignment:**
    *   **Supervision Standards:** **Arkansas Act 670 of 2025** (SB 485, signed April 2025) amends the law on suspended imposition of sentence, probation, parole and post-release supervision. It also creates a single evidence-based-practices and quality-assurance unit within the Department of Corrections. RENEW's tie of earned-release credits to Act 670 is **the repo's design claim** and has not been checked against the act's text. Act 659 of 2023, the Protect Arkansas Act, is a separate law.
    *   **Federal Deductions Ceiling:** Under the Prison Industry Enhancement Certification Program (**PIECP**, 18 U.S.C. § 1761(c)), wage deductions are capped at 80% of gross wages, and RENEW's 40/30/30 split is designed to fit inside that cap. *(The 80% figure was not re-verified in this pass.)*
    *   **AI Data Licensing & Model Ownership:** **Arkansas Act 927 of 2025** (HB 1876, signed April 21, 2025, effective August 2025) sets ownership rules for content and trained models produced with generative AI:
        *   the person who supplies the prompts or data owns the result, if the inputs were lawfully obtained and no contract says otherwise;
        *   under work-for-hire, the employer owns what an employee produces as part of the job;
        *   nobody can claim ownership of infringing content.

        It bears on licensing RENEW's telemetry-trained models. *(A claimed re-identification prohibition was removed; it could not be confirmed.)*

*   **Regulatory Docket:** **Arkansas Public Service Commission (APSC) Docket No. 26-001-U**, *In the Matter of the Application of Entergy Arkansas, LLC for Approval of Changes in Rates for Retail Electric Service.*
*   **Status & Schedule (official listings):**

    *   Administrative Law Judge Wesley Hunt presides.
    *   A **public comment hearing** was scheduled for **July 29, 2026**, and the **evidentiary hearing** for **November 4, 2026** (Arkansas.gov event listings).

*   **Rate Stabilization Fund Mechanics:** A reserve funded from state General Revenue surplus would shield residential customers from Fuel Adjustment Clause volatility and rider escalations. This is a policy proposal; APSC rate authority sits in **Ark. Code Ann. § 23-4-101 et seq.**

### The Six Branches & Critical Minerals
*   **Repo Docs:** [`commercial/the-six-branches.md`](commercial/the-six-branches.md), [`commercial/quartz-archive.md`](commercial/quartz-archive.md), [`commercial/quartz-5d-archival-spec.md`](commercial/quartz-5d-archival-spec.md)
*   **High-Purity Quartz (HPQ) Feasibility:**
    *   **Global Chokepoint:** Spruce Pine, NC supplies most of the world's semiconductor crucible-grade quartz.
    *   **Crucible thresholds:** the six-branches doc quotes Al < 5.5 ppm, Fe < 0.15 ppm, Ti < 3.1 ppm, total < 12.37 ppm and SiO₂ > 99.99%. These are **not** the IOTA standard, as an earlier draft said. For reference, IOTA-STD carries about 14 ppm Al, and IOTA-4/6/8 carry under 8 ppm.
    *   **Mount Ida Deposit:** Hydrothermal vein quartz, Montgomery County, AR. USGS Bull. 973-E and the Arkansas Geological Survey give qualitative ratings ("equal to Brazilian lascas") but no modern ICP-MS trace-element tables.
    *   **Actionable Next Step:** Commission a quartz-specific ICP-MS assay ($350–$600, 1–2 weeks). **No Mount Ida assay exists yet.**
    *   **5D Optical Glass Archive:** Data are not written into natural crystal. Its own birefringence drowns the written signal about 4,500:1 over 2 cm, and α-quartz turns to β-quartz at 573 °C. Feedstock quartz is melted into **fused quartz** (amorphous silica glass). See the spec's open item Q-09 on whether natural-quartz glass performs like optical-grade fused silica.
*   **Bauxite Residue REE Extraction:** The repo cites USGS work on gallium, niobium, scandium and rare earths in Saline and Pulaski county bauxites, plus DOE critical-materials funding. *Not re-verified in this pass.*
