## 1. Civic Layer Statutory & Code Verification

### Part I: ARMAWS (Arkansas Rural Mobility Air Standard)
*   **Repo Doc:** [`civic/armaws.md`](civic/armaws.md)
*   **Core Mandate:** Guarantees free compressed tire air at retail motor fuel dispensaries; prohibits paywalled air compressors ($0.75–$3.00) that cause tire failures, blowouts, and crashes among rural/low-income travelers.
*   **Statutory Alignment:**
    *   **Primary Legislative Model:** Connecticut General Statutes **§ 14-325a** (enacted 1979, requiring unconditional free air without requiring fuel purchase, minimum 80 psi, with mandatory pump signage).
    *   **Distinction from Failed Models:** California Business & Professions Code **§ 13651 (AB 531, 1999)** conditioned free air on fuel purchase, creating widespread non-compliance and enforcement loopholes.
    *   **Enforcement Authority:** Arkansas Department of Agriculture, **Bureau of Standards / Weights & Measures Division** (pursuant to **Ark. Code Ann. § 4-18-301 et seq.**), supplemented by the Arkansas State Police consumer hotline.
    *   **Funding Mechanism & Takings Safe Harbor:** Funded through the **Used Tire Recycling Fund** established under the **Used Tire Recycling and Accountability Act (Ark. Code Ann. § 8-9-401 et seq.)**, administered via the Tire Accountability Program (TAP). Allocating existing tire fee revenues to fund 100% equipment-retrofit grants for independent retailers eliminates Fifth Amendment regulatory takings exposure.

### Part II: Driver's License Public Access Guarantee Act
*   **Repo Doc:** [`civic/drivers-license-reform.md`](civic/drivers-license-reform.md)
*   **Core Mandate:** Eliminates the "Mobility Trap" wherein applicants must provide a legally registered, insured, compliant private vehicle to take the state driving skills test.
*   **Statutory Alignment:**
    *   **State Law vs. Administrative Rule:** Under **Ark. Code Ann. § 27-16-704** (*Examinations of Applicants*) and **§ 27-16-705** (*Examiners*), the General Assembly mandates vision, knowledge, and skills testing, but is completely silent regarding vehicle provision. The private vehicle mandate is solely an administrative policy of the Department of Finance and Administration (DFA) / Arkansas State Police (ASP) Driver Testing Division.
    *   **Fleet Sourcing & Existing Revolving Fund:** Fleet procurement is authorized under **Ark. Code Ann. § 22-8-206** (*Automobile and Pickup Truck Acquisition Act for the State of Arkansas*) through the Motor Vehicle Acquisition Revolving Fund. State vehicle acquisitions and agency transfers operate under DFA oversight.
    *   **Fiscal Scope:** Sourcing surplus state units prior to public auction and roadworthy civilly forfeited vehicles under **Ark. Code Ann. § 5-64-505** ensures near-zero marginal taxpayer capital expenditure. Liability coverage integrates into the Arkansas State and Public School Employee Self-Insurance / State Vehicle Risk Management Program under **Ark. Code Ann. § 21-5-701 et seq.**

### Part III: AINSA (Arkansas Infant Nutrition Security Act)
*   **Repo Doc:** [`civic/infant-nutrition-security-act.md`](civic/infant-nutrition-security-act.md)
*   **Core Mandate:** Eliminates the "Month-End Formula Gap" for rural infants on WIC (3–4 uncovered days per month).
*   **Federal & State Regulatory Grounding:**
    *   **Federal MMA Regulation:** Codified at **7 CFR § 246.10** (*Supplemental Foods*), Table 1. Fully formula-fed infants aged 0–3 months receive a Maximum Monthly Allowance (MMA) of 806 reconstituted fluid ounces (or 9 cans of 12.4–12.5 oz powder).
    *   **Actual Pediatric Appetite:** American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) feeding standard specifies 2.5 oz per pound of body weight per day (24–32 oz/day for healthy newborns; 30 oz/day × 30 days = 900 oz/month, yielding a 94 oz / ~3–4 day deficit).
    *   **Sole-Source Distortion:** USDA Economic Research Service (ERS) reports confirm WIC sole-source manufacturer rebate contracts routinely exceed 85–95% of wholesale price (with March 2023 contracts exceeding 100% of wholesale). GAO-25 reports indicate winning contract brands capture 71–80% of state retail market share, with retail spillover price increases of ~1.7% to non-WIC shoppers.
    *   **Statutory Honesty Clause:** AINSA does not alter 7 CFR § 246.10 federal rules (which would require a federal USDA waiver). Instead, it enacts an independent **State Supplemental Infant Nutrition Bridge Fund** administered by Arkansas Department of Human Services (DHS) Division of County Operations, redeemed on the state WIC EBT card via state General Revenue surplus.

### Part IV: Project RENEW (ADC Reentry & Environmental Remediation)
*   **Repo Doc:** [`civic/project-renew.md`](civic/project-renew.md)
*   **Core Mandate:** Compensated, voluntary Arkansas Division of Correction (ADC) work crews conducting highway litter removal and materials recovery, paired with an escrow wealth engine (40% locked release escrow, 30% restitution/family, 30% commissary).
*   **Statutory Alignment:**
    *   **Earned-Release & Supervision Standards:** Fully aligned with **Arkansas Act 670 of 2025**, which overhauled community supervision, post-prison transfer assessments, and evidence-based rehabilitation metrics to slash recidivism. (Distinguished from Act 659 of 2023, the Protect Arkansas Act, which sets mandatory minimums for violent felonies).
    *   **Federal Deductions Ceiling:** Federal Prison Industry Enhancement Certification Program (**PIECP**, 18 U.S.C. § 1761(c)) restricts cumulative wage deductions to a maximum of **80% of gross wages**. Project RENEW's 40/30/30 distribution fits squarely inside statutory federal limits.
    *   **AI Data Licensing & Model Ownership:** Autonomous drone routing and computer vision telemetry operate under **Arkansas Act 927 of 2025** (HB 1876, effective August 5, 2025), which codifies legal ownership rights for trained AI models and prompts, explicitly protecting entity model IP while prohibiting re-identification of personal data.
