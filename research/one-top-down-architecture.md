## 0. What this actually is

Not a holding company of startups. A **wholonomy**: a regional industrial and civic loop that converts Arkansas liabilities (curtailed watts, tires, residues, reentry, unpaid air, license traps) into inputs for the next node, and returns a visible public remainder.

**Plausibility, in one sentence:** the civic on-ramps (free air, license access) are legally ordinary and cheap; the mineral and compute trunk are real *as research and interconnection problems*, not as already-bankable mines or data centers.

## 2. Operating mechanisms (more durable than names)

1. **State-pays-first** — use an existing fee/surplus/appropriation for the first cost.  
2. **Liability-flip** — take a bounded waste/site in exchange for access or a share.  
3. **Fence-line arbitrage** — value at the physical edge of a generator or mill.  
4. **Software yield** — route people, lots, trucks; don't invent a new industry.  
5. **Labor-pipeline-as-payoff** — credentials + real employers, not certificates.  
6. **Provenance ledger** — material, labor, public money, energy.

## 3. Immediate civic track (does not wait for ONE)

### 3.1 ARMAWS — free compressed air

- **Legal analog:** California BPC § 13651.  
- **Arkansas hook:** tire-recycling fee / ADEQ TAP (confirm dedication and surplus before claiming a fund). `[O]` balance.  
- **Build:** calibrated gauges, signage, enforcement, rural alt sites (co-op yards, libraries, county shops, mobile).  
- **Orchard membrane:** service geography → recoveRY tire collection map.  
- **Startup shape `[conj]`:** statutory one-pager + 10-county pilot with ADEQ reporting. Not a new tax.

### 3.2 Driver's license testing fleet

- **First work:** rule-versus-law audit of Arkansas State Police examiner/vehicle rules.  
- **Build:** mobile testing days at regional depots; document-recovery help; CDL prep; 90-day employer shuttle (shared with RENEW).  
- **Why it is structural:** RENEW graduates who cannot test cannot drive into deliveRY jobs.

Do these even if ONE never interconnects.

## 4. Commercial trunk — startup sequence (phase gates)

**Phase 0 — Paper that can be audited (now–90 days)**  
Rights-and-evidence registry in libraRY. Option templates. Public-benefit covenant language. Claim-by-claim IP docket (resolve 79 vs ~112). `[O]` USPTO.

**Phase 1 — Measurement businesses (90 days–12 months)**  
- Tire collection + ARMAWS geography (10 counties).  
- Bauxite residue characterization with USGS methods, not a mine plan.  
- Quartz quality registry: specimen → ICP-MS / LA-ICP-MS vs crucible / optical / piezo specs.  
- Freight-intelligence overlay on the existing State Freight Plan (6,800+ logistics firms already in-state).  
- One poultry-litter cluster mass-balance (struvite / biogas) with a contracted radius.

**Phase 2 — ONE interconnect study (parallel, 6–24 months)**  
Entergy Arkansas / MISO / ANO fence-line: interruptible tariff, substation, water, noise, security. **No campus construction until interconnection + ratepayer protection are written.** RCD must be a contract clause, not a slogan.

**Phase 3 — factoRY / memoRY hardware**  
CNC cell for **modular server racks**, coil housings, sample fixtures. Containerized edge node at one co-op substation. This is the “racks I designed six months ago” fruit.

**Phase 4 — RENEW pilot**  
Voluntary, paid, independently monitored. No public recidivism percentages until that study exists.

**Phase 5 — ConservatoRY**  
501(c)(3) after a revenue node exists. ForestRY plantings on recoveRY/reclamation parcels; TreasuRY scholarships; community collection days.

## 6. Who to call (collaboration and data)

### Geology / materials

- **USGS** — bauxite reconnaissance (already cited); do not over-read REE.  
- **Arkansas Geological Survey / ANRC** — quartz districts, mine-land inventory.  
- **University of Arkansas (Fayetteville)** — chemical engineering, poultry science (litter), Institute for Nanoscience (materials).  
- **UA Little Rock / UA System** — applied chemistry, workforce.  
- **NETL / DOE critical-minerals programs** — process R&D, not a mine permit.  
- **Quartz / silica buyers' QA labs** (optical, fused-quartz, piezo) — *falsify* crucible-grade claims; do not shop for a confirmatory lab.

### Energy / compute

- **Entergy Arkansas**, **APSC**, **MISO** — interruptible load, ratepayer protection (Docket 26-001-U is already in the civic memory file).  
- **Arkansas Electric Cooperatives / AECC** — rural substations for memoRY nodes and ARMAWS power.  
- **ANO site owner/operator counsel** — fence-line is a security and NRC-adjacent conversation; do not amateur-hour it.

### Civic / labor

- **ADEQ** — Tire Accountability Program.  
- **Arkansas State Police** — examiner rules.  
- **ADC** — RENEW only as voluntary partnership; no unpaid carceral labor.  
- **County judges / co-ops** — alt air sites, depot land.

### Logistics / manufacturing

- Existing **6,800+** freight firms — intelligence layer, not a new trucking company.  
- Regional CNC / pressure-vessel fabricators — factoRY overflow until in-house cell is real.  
- Modular-rack OEMs (the market RY noted is catching up) — **buy vs build** comparison in Phase 3, with foundRY IP as the differentiator if claims survive docketing.

### ConservatoRY

- County extension / forestry; reclamation bond holders; school districts for TreasuRY scholarships later.

Treat the fortress as a **governance instrument** (standing to enforce loop rules) only after a claim chart exists: family, filing number, status, embodiment (Gentle Authority, DWPT, hydrometallurgy, ledger, rack/chassis). Until then, “79” and “112” are **corpus counts**, not USPTO facts.

## 8. One breathing cycle (so it is not a bland org chart)

ANO off-peak watt → ONE interruptible load → RCD to county microgrid → ARMAWS air point stays powered → tire referral → recoveRY lot → libraRY passport → refineRY or crumb rubber → deliveRY to buyer → factoRY builds the next fixture and rack → memoRY sits in that rack at a co-op → RENEW apprentice signed off in libraRY drives the next lot → ConservatoRY plants the recoveRY berm.

Remove ARMAWS or the license fleet and rural workers never enter. Remove libraRY and buyers won't take the lot. Remove the curtailment covenant and the trunk is just another extractive tenant.
