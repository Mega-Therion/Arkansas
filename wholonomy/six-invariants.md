# The six Wholonomy invariants

"**Wholonomy = whole economy × whole holonomy.**" The design test applied to every piece of the
portfolio: traversing its own loop and returning must carry the whole's signature. Six invariants:

1. **Gentle Authority** — Segment → Authenticate → Consent → Tune → Promote Slowly → Power-Down.
2. **Beneficial to everyone** — not just shareholders; the traveler with the slow leak, the
   reentry hire, the county that gets the plant.
3. **Closed value loop** — dividends recycle through foundRY's Inverse Bitcoin DRIP instead of
   leaving the ground.
4. **Arkansas-first** — the state the tree is rooted in eats the fruit first.
5. **Redundancy by design** — six branches, no single point of failure.
6. **Waste → wealth** — recoveRY and refineRY turn liability sites into feedstock for factoRY and
   memoRY.

## Strategic sequencing (as reasoned through, not yet executed)

The three civic initiatives are not treated as three unrelated bills but one thesis, staged
deliberately:

- **ARMAWS and the Driver's-License Fleet go first** — small, fast, cheap, easy to explain. They
  prove the state can absorb a cost without a new tax.
- **The power plan (Rate Stabilization Fund) follows** — once the first two have passed, it's not
  a radical idea; it's the third thing the same philosophy already produced, and the first two
  already worked.
- **The messaging discipline:** a "mirror" message — an open question ("what have we become") —
  goes into public discourse first, with no name attached and no answer supplied. The plan (the
  bills, the funding model, the Diamond Foundation) exists complete *before* the mirror goes up,
  so there's an answer ready the moment someone looks and doesn't like what they see. Deliberately
  not run as a single-founder campaign — each initiative is designed to "walk out on its own legs"
  to its own sponsor/agency rather than carry one person's name, to keep the work bigger than any
  one architect.
