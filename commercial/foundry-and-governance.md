## Gentle Authority Doctrine

The governance "immune system" the whole tree operates under, ensuring growth stays aligned with
local trust rather than outrunning it:

**Segment → Authenticate → Consent → Tune → Promote Slowly → Power-Down.**

This is the system-facing half of the portfolio's philosophy. The **Diamond Foundation** (see
[`../wholonomy/diamond-foundation.md`](../wholonomy/diamond-foundation.md)) is its citizen-facing
counterpart — the state stands in front of the citizen and the structure stays legible; Gentle
Authority is how the Orchard governs itself so it never becomes the thing it replaced.
