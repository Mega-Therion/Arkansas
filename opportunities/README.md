# Candidate opportunities — not yet part of the portfolio

Unlike the rest of this repo, nothing in this directory is RY's own established plan. These are
candidates surfaced by a 2026-09-11 cross-domain "connections" pass: take the reusable mechanisms
already proven in the portfolio, and pattern-match them against real, externally-verified Arkansas
facts to find domains those mechanisms haven't been applied to yet. Everything here is a starting
point to evaluate, not a commitment.

## The mechanisms, named for the first time

RY has independently invented the same few mechanisms multiple times without naming them as a
reusable playbook:

1. **State-pays-first** — a surplus or existing fund absorbs a cost before the citizen/business
   does. Used three times already: ARMAWS's air fund, the Driver's-License fleet (via Act 1006's
   existing appropriation), and the Entergy Rate Stabilization Fund.
2. **Liability-flip** — an environmental/regulatory liability becomes an asset by taking it off an
   owner's hands, rather than paying for feedstock (recoveRY/refineRY on mine tailings).
3. **Rule-vs-law audit** — find restrictions people assume are statute but are actually just agency
   policy, fixable without full legislation (the driver's-license vehicle requirement).
4. **Fence-line arbitrage** — co-locate value capture directly at a power/resource source (ONE +
   Arkansas Nuclear One).
5. **AI-optimization over physical logistics** — OmegA layered onto deliveRY/RENEW routing.
6. **Labor-pipeline-as-payoff** — turn a cost center into a workforce bridge (RENEW's escrow model).
