# Occupational-licensing non-compliance

**Status:** candidate, not RY's plan — surfaced 2026-09-11.
**Mechanism:** #3 (rule-vs-law audit), but sharper — this isn't a rule mistaken for law, it's
agencies demonstrably ignoring a real law that already passed.

## The finding

Arkansas passed a universal occupational-licensing recognition law (Ark. Code §17-7-105) —
in principle, it lets people with an out-of-state license get recognized here without starting
over. But the Arkansas Legislative Council's own FY2024-25 reporting shows **four Department of
Agriculture boards accepted zero licenses** under it.
[Source](https://arkleg.state.ar.us/Home/FTPDocument?path=/Assembly/Meeting+Attachments/040/27518/A.+Summary+Agenda_June+19+2025.pdf)

Separately, Arkansas ranks as the **9th-most-burdensome licensing state** nationally — 71% of
sampled occupations licensed, versus a 53% national average.
[Source](https://uca.edu/acre/2023/02/10/where-does-arkansas-stand-on-universal-licensing-recognition-and-how-can-it-improve/)

## Why this is different from the driver's-license discovery

The driver's-license vehicle requirement was an administrative rule that people mistake for law —
fixable by changing the rule. This is the opposite pattern: **a real law already exists, and
agencies aren't following it.** That means the lever isn't new legislation — it's compliance and
accountability. Cheaper to fix, and there's already an official paper trail (the Legislative
Council's own reporting) documenting the non-compliance, rather than needing to build the case
from scratch the way ARMAWS or the License-fleet bill did.

## Open questions before this becomes a real plan

- Which four Agriculture boards specifically, and what's their stated reason (if any) for zero
  acceptances?
- Is this pattern isolated to Agriculture, or does it show up across other licensing boards too?
- What's the actual enforcement mechanism for §17-7-105 — is there one, or is that the real gap?
- Who would the sympathetic-constituency story be here (the ARMAWS "two bald tires" equivalent) —
  a specific tradesperson or professional who moved to Arkansas and got stonewalled?
