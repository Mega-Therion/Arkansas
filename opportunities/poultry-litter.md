# Poultry litter — biogas & struvite

**Status:** candidate, not RY's plan — surfaced 2026-09-11.
**Mechanism:** #2 (liability-flip) + #4 (fence-line arbitrage), same logic as ONE+ANO and
recoveRY/refineRY on mine tailings, applied to a completely different feedstock and region.

## The finding

Arkansas produces an estimated **1.3 million metric tons of poultry litter per year**,
concentrated in the Northwest Arkansas poultry belt — a different region from ONE's Russellville
siting or refineRY's central-Arkansas bauxite/mineral focus.
[Source](https://aaes.uada.edu/research-highlights/poultry-litter-digestion-system/)

A liquid-state anaerobic digester — producing biogas plus struvite fertilizer as a byproduct — is
running as a prototype at the Arkansas Agricultural Experiment Station, described as the first of
its kind in the US. It's still academic, not commercialized.
[Source](https://www.uaex.uada.edu/media-resources/news/2023/april/04-28-2023-ark-poultry-litter-digester-update.aspx)

## Why this fits the existing playbook

Poultry litter is currently a disposal problem for producers (an environmental liability, same
shape as mine tailings) sitting on top of a real energy value (biogas — same shape as ONE's
fence-line baseload arbitrage). The science already exists and is proven at prototype scale; the
business layer commercializing it at Arkansas's actual poultry-industry scale does not appear to
exist yet.

## Open questions before this becomes a real plan

- Who currently bears the disposal cost/liability for poultry litter — individual growers, the
  integrators (Tyson, etc.), or the state — and who would recoveRY-style liability-flip actually
  be negotiating with?
- What's the real commercial-scale economics of the digester technology, beyond the prototype?
- Does this compete with or complement existing poultry-litter uses (it's already used as
  fertilizer in some cases) — is there actually unclaimed value here, or is the "problem" partially
  already solved by existing practice?
- Would this sit under a new branch, or extend recoveRY/refineRY's existing scope?
